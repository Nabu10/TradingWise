The idea

Once your position is up by your target gain, sell only the slice of shares whose proceeds equal what you originally paid. The remaining shares have zero dollars left in them — any further loss can't take back money you already recovered.

Reading the numbers

"Exact" is the fractional share count that recovers your cost precisely. "Shares to sell" rounds that up to a whole share, since most brokers won't sell partial shares — so the surplus is usually a few dollars above zero, not exactly zero.

Worth knowing

This is a position-sizing technique, not investment advice, and it says nothing about whether a stock is worth holding. Taxes on the sale aren't included here — check your own numbers before trading.
